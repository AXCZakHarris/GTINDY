iLIFE claim details

Unit basis

15 min matches the IRIS Provider Invoice (rate ÷ 4). HR bills hours at the CSV hourly rate.

Rules
  • One form per participant per calendar month.
  • Same-day visits for a participant are merged into a single line.
  • Service dates per page: 9 on the IRIS Provider Invoice, 14 on the iLIFE Vendor Claim Form. Longer months overflow into _pt2, _pt3, …
  • Units default to 15-minute increments at the equivalent unit rate.
  • On the iLIFE form, signature lines and any ID the CSV doesn't carry are left blank for hand-completion.
