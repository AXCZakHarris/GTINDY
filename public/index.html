AxisCare → IRIS Provider Invoice

Upload an AxisCare ClaimBatchCreation CSV. You'll get back one IRIS Provider Invoice PDF per participant per service month.

Rules
  • One invoice per participant per calendar month.
  • Same-day visits for a participant are merged into a single line.
  • Up to 9 service dates per page; longer months overflow into _pt2, _pt3, …
  • Units are reported in 15-minute increments at the equivalent unit rate.
This tool handles Protected Health Information (PHI). Your file is processed entirely inside this browser tab — it is never uploaded, saved, or sent to anyone. Do not share generated PDFs outside of authorized billing workflows.
